YouTube Channel & Content Policy

Terms of Service, Copyright Compliance & Audience Guidelines for Video Channels

Effective Date: October 2026 Platform: YouTube (Google LLC) Contact: neilstudio.feedback@gmail.com
Audience & Copyright Pledge: Neil Studio publishes digital video broadcasts on YouTube with strict adherence to YouTube Community Guidelines, Google Terms of Service, respect for creator copyright, and protective measures for viewer privacy.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to all long-form videos, YouTube Shorts, Community tab updates, and associated interactive features produced and broadcast under Neil Studio's official YouTube network.

2. Compliance with Google & YouTube Regulations

We operate in full accordance with host platform rules:

  • YouTube Terms of Service: Complying with account integrity, age eligibility, and service usage terms.
  • Community Guidelines: Zero publication of harmful, dangerous, sexually explicit, hateful, violent, or harassing material.
  • Made for Kids Policy (COPPA): Strict classification of target audiences for all uploads, adhering to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act.

3. Viewer Privacy & Data Safeguards

  • No Deceptive External Links: We never attach malicious shorteners, adware trackers, or unauthorized data-capturing forms within video descriptions or pinned comments.
  • Personal Privacy Protection: Individuals featured in video broadcasts are presented in accordance with public filming laws or explicit consent.
  • Viewer Discretion: Viewers possess complete autonomy over their watch history and comment contributions via standard YouTube account controls.

4. Copyright, Licensing & Fair Use

Neil Studio respects the legal intellectual property rights of all musicians, artists, animators, and digital creators:

  • Background scores and audio assets are obtained via authorized commercial licenses or the YouTube Audio Library.
  • In instances involving commentary, critiques, reviews, parody, or news analysis, we operate under the doctrine of Fair Use pursuant to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).
  • Amicable Dispute & Quick Resolution Process: If you are a copyright owner or authorized representative and believe any portion of our video material warrants credit adjustment or removal:
    Please reach out directly to our dedicated desk at: neilstudio.feedback@gmail.com prior to issuing formal copyright strikes.
    We guarantee prompt review and amicable remediation (video modification, credit update, or complete takedown) within 24 to 48 business hours.

5. Commercial Transparency & Monetization

As members of the YouTube Partner Program (YPP), our channels adhere to Google's AdSense and monetization policies:

  • We strictly prohibit artificial view manipulation, click fraud, or deceptive thumbnail clickbait.
  • Any sponsored collaboration or paid brand promotion prominently activates YouTube's official "Includes paid promotion" notification for viewer transparency.
